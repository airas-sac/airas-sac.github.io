ACM SAC 2027 · Technical Track

AI-enabled Resilience for
Autonomous Systems

Bringing together researchers from systems security, machine learning, control theory, and dependable computing to advance the resilience of AI-driven autonomous platforms.

Scope of AIRAS

Autonomous systems — ground and aerial vehicles, cognitive robots, satellite constellations, and industrial control platforms — are increasingly governed by AI-driven components responsible for perception, planning, and actuation.

This shift creates a distinctive resilience challenge: AI components can fail or be manipulated in ways that classical fault-tolerance and security mechanisms were not designed to handle.

Research on AI-driven resilience for autonomous systems draws from systems security, machine learning, control theory, and dependable computing. AIRAS foregrounds AI as the instrument of resilience — the means by which an autonomous system detects, tolerates, adapts to, and recovers from failures and attacks, operating at the system level.

We invite original research papers, experience reports, and contributions on emerging results related to the security and resilience of AI-driven autonomous systems. Papers will be a maximum of 8 pages of new, previously unpublished work.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Adversarial Robustness: Runtime defenses preserving system-level operation across perception, planning, and control pipelines.
  • AI-driven Threat Detection: Anomaly detection, intrusion detection, and adaptive threat response mechanisms for autonomous systems.
  • Explainable AI for Safety: XAI techniques for resilience assurance and safety verification of autonomous operations.
  • Real-time System Security: Attack and defense mechanisms for learning-enabled real-time and safety-critical systems.
  • Federated & Privacy-preserving Learning: Resilient distributed learning paradigms for coordinated autonomous systems.
  • Adaptive Self-healing: Reinforcement learning for adaptive and self-healing autonomous behavior under disturbances.
  • Runtime Assurance & Monitoring: Uncertainty-aware monitoring to keep safety-critical AI within safe operating envelopes.
  • Foundation & Large Language Models: Leveraging LLMs and foundation models for cyber-physical and autonomous systems resiliency.
  • Fault Diagnosis & Recovery: AI-enabled fault diagnosis, prognosis, and automated system recovery strategies.
  • Trust & Assurance Frameworks: End-to-end trust and assurance frameworks for AI-driven autonomous decision-making.
  • Hardware/Software Co-design: Resilient AI architectures for embedded and edge platforms with resource constraints.
  • Domain-specific Resilience: Autonomous vehicles, drones, robots, space systems, and industrial cyber-physical systems.
  • Secure Model Deployment: Integrity assurance and secure deployment pipelines for AI models in the field.
  • Multi-agent Coordination: Resilient multi-agent coordination and consensus under adversarial conditions.
  • Quantum Computing for Autonomy: Safe integration of quantum computing and quantum machine learning in autonomous systems.
  • Digital Twins & Simulation: Simulation-based resilience analysis using digital twin methodologies.
  • Benchmarks & Evaluation: Testbeds, benchmarks, and evaluation methodologies for AI resilience research.
  • Resource-constrained AI Security: AI security tailored for safety-critical platforms with strict resource budgets.
