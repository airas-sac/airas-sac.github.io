# AI-enabled Resilience for Autonomous Systems (AIRAS)

Autonomous systems are increasingly built on AI components, perception, planning, and control models that learn from data rather than follow fixed rules. This brings new capability, but also new failure modes: adversarial inputs, distribution shift, and inference-time attacks that classical fault-tolerance and security techniques were never built to catch. As autonomous vehicles, drones, robots, and industrial control systems take on safety- and mission-critical roles, these AI-induced failures carry consequences that go well beyond the system itself.

AIRAS (AI-enabled Resilience for Autonomous Systems) is an ongoing initiative bringing together the research and practice needed to address this challenge, spanning conference tracks, workshops, and resources for researchers and practitioners working across AI, systems security, control theory, and dependable computing.
